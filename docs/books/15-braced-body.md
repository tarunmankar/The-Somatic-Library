## 🎯 Quick Overview & CRO Hooks
* **Primary Emotional Hook:** *"Even sitting quietly on your sofa, your body feels braced for a blow."*
* **Follow-up Hook:** Clenched jaw, tight shoulders, held breath. That's not poor posture — it's your nervous system in fortress mode.
* **Target Audience:** Adults aged 24-52 suffering from chronic unconscious physical bracing, persistent clenched jaw (TMJ/bruxism), hiked-up shoulders, locked diaphragm, contracted pelvic floor, and inability to soften muscles.
* **Real-World Trigger Scenario:** Sitting at a stoplight or typing an ordinary email, suddenly realizing your shoulders are raised up to your ears, your molars are clamped together, and you have not taken a full breath in twenty minutes.
* **Primary Somatic Anchor:** Clenched masseter jaw muscles, rigid suboccipital neck tendons, locked immobile ribcage, tightly contracted psoas and pelvic floor.
* **Autonomic Nervous System State:** Persistent Somatosensory Threat Neuroception and Chronic Neuromuscular Armoring (The Motor-System Bracing Reflex).

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Chronic physical bracing is an evolutionary survival defense known as neuromuscular armoring. Willpower relaxation fails because the armoring is involuntary. Tarun Mankar delivers immediate physical protocols to dissolve muscle armor at the root — through pandicular resets, jaw and suboccipital de-armoring, and vagal safety anchors — to retrain sensorimotor amnesia.

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Your somatic, science-backed roadmap to healing anxious attachment. Provides practical, in-the-moment tools to silence relationship hypervigilance, calm acute panic in ninety seconds, and step off the exhausting pursuer-distancer treadmill. Learn to communicate needs with grounded vulnerability, distinguish genuine red flags from internal anxiety triggers, and build deep peaceful intimacy.

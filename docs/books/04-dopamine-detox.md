## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Constantly checking your phone, struggling to focus, or feeling mentally exhausted by midday? Modern digital environments are engineered to hijack your brain's reward pathways. This audiobook gives you a clear roadmap to reclaim your mind — practical bodily tools, digital hygiene protocols, and daily habits to lower your dopamine baseline and restore effortless concentration.

## 🎯 Quick Overview & CRO Hooks
* **Primary Emotional Hook:** *"Checking your bank balance makes you feel physically sick?"*
* **Follow-up Hook:** It's not a budgeting problem. Your body learned to fear money before your mind did.
* **Target Audience:** Individuals, professionals, and entrepreneurs who experience visceral terror, panic, or hoarding urges around money despite earning a stable income.
* **Real-World Trigger Scenario:** Logging into a banking app and feeling physical nausea before the balance loads; feeling sick when paying for necessary car repairs; agonizing for three days over buying groceries.
* **Primary Somatic Anchor:** Cold visceral knot in the solar plexus/gut, sudden dry swallow, tight contracted diaphragm whenever credit card is tapped or an invoice arrives.
* **Autonomic Nervous System State:** Survival Threat Neuroception triggered by Resource Insecurity, Poverty Memory, and Financial Vulnerability.

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Stare at your bank app, physically frozen with anxiety? Feel a tight chest when paying routine bills? You aren't bad with money — your body is stuck in a survival loop. This audiobook translates somatic regulation into simple everyday financial habits. Learn the science of interoceptive alarms, use sixty-second nervous system resets before opening spreadsheets, and expand your capacity to hold wealth.

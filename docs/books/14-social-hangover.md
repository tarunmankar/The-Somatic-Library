## 🎯 Quick Overview & CRO Hooks

* **Real-World Trigger Scenario:** Getting into the car after a three-hour dinner party; the smile falls off the face, exhaustion hits like a brick, and the brain immediately starts an autopsy: 'Did I sound stupid when I said that?'

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
The social hangover is an autonomic survival state caused by sensory saturation, social masking, and an unbudgeted nervous system. Tarun Mankar reveals why your brain replays conversations like a courtroom trial and delivers immediate physical protocols to discharge trapped tension, disarm cringe attacks, set unhurried departure anchors, and walk into any room with calm sovereignty.

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Stare at your screen for hours, physically exhausted yet mentally wired? Run on caffeine and collapse at night? Your nervous system is stuck in a chronic threat loop. Somatic Pacing is your body-first blueprint to reclaim daily focus and energy — learn sixty-second resets, vagus nerve techniques, and energy boundaries to clear afternoon brain fog without sacrificing output.

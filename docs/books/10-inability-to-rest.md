## 🎯 Quick Overview & CRO Hooks
* **Primary Emotional Hook:** *"You sit down to relax and your chest tightens with unexplained panic."*
* **Follow-up Hook:** You compulsively scrub counters, unable to survive ten minutes of stillness. You tell yourself you're driven. But you don't know how to stop.
* **Target Audience:** Driven professionals, perfectionists, and chronic over-achievers who experience physical panic, restlessness, guilt, and dread whenever they try to sit still or take a day off.
* **Real-World Trigger Scenario:** Sitting on the living room rug on a sunny Sunday afternoon with no obligations; feeling a creeping wave of guilt, jumping up to vacuum the rug or check work emails.
* **Primary Somatic Anchor:** Restless buzzing in the legs, tight vibrating skin across the chest, internal motor that will not idle, inability to settle spine into the chair.
* **Autonomic Nervous System State:** Sympathetic Lock-in with Vagal Withdrawal and Biological Rest Intolerance.

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Your nervous system equates stillness with danger. Chronic busyness is not ambition — it is survival armor. This audiobook reveals how to re-engage your vagal brake, disarm downtime guilt, dissolve the fawning reflex, and protect your white space without apologizing. You do not need to earn your right to pause.

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Your nervous system misclassifies harmless biological noise as fatal emergencies. The Phantom Alarm delivers the definitive somatic roadmap to breaking interoceptive hypervigilance. Discover step-by-step resets to downshift racing heart rates, decouple physical sensations from catastrophic stories, and end compulsive symptom checking. Stop treating your body like a ticking bomb.

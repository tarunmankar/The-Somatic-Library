## 🎯 Quick Overview & CRO Hooks
* **Primary Emotional Hook:** *"Sent a text 45 minutes ago and your chest is still burning?"*
* **Follow-up Hook:** Your nervous system is stuck in a threat loop — and reading more affirmations won't fix it.
* **Target Audience:** Adults aged 24-42 dealing with relationship anxiety, text overthinking, and fear of sudden abandonment.
* **Real-World Trigger Scenario:** Watching a sent text message sit without a reply for forty-five minutes; noticing a three-word response instead of the usual emoji; interpreting a subtle shift in a partner's tone during dinner.
* **Primary Somatic Anchor:** Sharp visceral heat in the center of the chest, tight throat clamp, compulsive trembling in fingers tapping the phone screen, shallow breath.
* **Autonomic Nervous System State:** Sympathetic Hyper-Arousal driven by Relational Abandonment Threat & Attachment Panic.

---

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Check your phone constantly when a text goes unanswered? Overthink shifts in tone and worry closeness will disappear? Your nervous system is stuck in a hyperactive threat loop. Stop Spiraling is your practical toolkit to break the cycle of anxious attachment — regulate your nervous system, tolerate uncertainty, and communicate clearly using honest relationship scripts that build closeness.

---

## 🧠 Core Transformation & Proprietary Protocols
* **Core Transformation:** From frantic reassurance-seeking and text spirals to grounded self-trust, emotional pauses, and clear spoken communication.
* **Key Body-First Protocols:**
  - **The Sixty-Second Relational De-escalation Breath**
  - **The Thought-Stopping Sensory Tap**
  - **The Non-Urgent Response Buffer**
  - **The Text-Panic Physical Reset**
  - **The Calm Boundary Relationship Script**

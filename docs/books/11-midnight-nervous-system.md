## 🎯 Quick Overview & CRO Hooks
* **Primary Emotional Hook:** *"Your head hits the pillow and your heart starts pounding."*
* **Follow-up Hook:** You snap awake at 3 AM calculating how many hours until dawn. You've tried everything. Your body treats the dark like an interrogation room.
* **Target Audience:** Adults and professionals who lie awake in the dark at 2:00 AM or 3:00 AM with a racing pulse, dreading the morning alarm, feeling like the bed has become an interrogation room.
* **Real-World Trigger Scenario:** Turning off the bedside lamp, closing eyes, and having a wave of catastrophic adrenaline surge through the ribs; looking at the clock at 3:14 AM and doing sleep math.
* **Primary Somatic Anchor:** Pounding pulse in the temples audible on the pillow, cold sweat on palms, hyper-alert auditory scanning of silence, locked jaw in the dark.
* **Autonomic Nervous System State:** Nocturnal Sympathetic Hyper-Arousal, Nocturnal Cortisol Dysregulation, and Sleep Threat Neuroception.

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Your autonomic nervous system perceives bedtime as a threat. Grounded in polyvagal neuroscience and somatic medicine, this audiobook provides lying-down physical resets, clock-panic interrupts, and vagal downshifting protocols to discharge trapped motor tension without fighting your mind. You do not need to force sleep — just teach your body it is safe to rest.

* **Core Transformation:** From midnight panic, bed dread, and frantic sleep performance pressure to effortless lying-down downshifting, biological safety in darkness, and restful deep sleep.

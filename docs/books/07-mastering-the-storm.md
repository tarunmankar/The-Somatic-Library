## 🎯 Quick Overview & CRO Hooks
* **Primary Emotional Hook:** *"Minor annoyances make your blood boil in seconds?"*
* **Follow-up Hook:** A flash of anger makes you say things you instantly regret. That's not your character — it's a survival hijack.
* **Target Audience:** Individuals who struggle with sudden explosive anger, irritability, emotional flooding, road rage, defensive snapping, or regretting spoken words under pressure.
* **Real-World Trigger Scenario:** Being interrupted during a high-stakes discussion, being cut off in traffic, or feeling unfairly accused; experiencing an instantaneous zero-to-sixty explosion of fury.
* **Primary Somatic Anchor:** Surging heat up the neck and face, clenched fists, grinding molars/jaw, tunnel vision, pounding heavy pulse in the ears.
* **Autonomic Nervous System State:** High-Arousal Sympathetic Fight Activation and Acute Amygdala Hijack.

## 📝 Book Synopsis & Description
Your somatic, science-backed roadmap to regaining complete self-command. Reveals how to halt emotional flooding at the neurochemical level before it dictates your words. Detect subtle bodily warning signs, cool down sympathetic surges in ninety seconds with vagal resets, and replace instant defensiveness with unshakable inner poise.

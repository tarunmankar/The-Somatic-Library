## PART 1: HOW TO MAKE AI WRITE LIKE A REAL HUMAN THERAPIST / AUTHOR
*(Bypassing AI Detectors, Beating Google Helpful Content Update, and Delivering Full Emotional "Feel")*

### 1. The Anatomy of Generic/Boring AI Writing (What We MUST AVOID)
Generic AI content fails on Google and loses readers within 5 seconds because of:
- **Abstract generalizations:** "Stress is common in modern life and affects many people." *(Zero emotion)*
- **Cliché robotic transition phrases:** "In today's fast-paced world...", "Delve into...", "It's important to note...", "In conclusion...", "Furthermore..."
- **Intellectualizing the body:** Treating emotions as mere thoughts rather than neurochemical bodily sensations.
- **Uniform sentence length:** Robotic rhythm without cadence, pauses, or breath.

### 2. The "Visceral Somatic" Writing Framework (How to Prompt & Write)
Every article must follow the **Interoceptive Human Voice**:
1. **Sensory Grounding (First Sentence):** Start inside the physical body, not with an abstract definition.
   - *Example:* "Your phone sits face-down on the counter. It has been forty-seven minutes since you hit send. Your throat feels dry, your shoulders are pinned to your ears, and there is a burning knot right below your ribcage."
2. **De-Shaming Mechanism:** Dispel the reader's self-blame by substituting psychological shame with biological reality.
   - *Example:* "You tell yourself you are crazy. You tell yourself to just breathe. But your prefrontal cortex went offline thirty minutes ago—because to an evolutionary attachment alarm, silence registers as biological abandonment."
3. **Conversational Cadence & Rhythm:** Vary sentence lengths drastically. Use 3-word punchy lines followed by a deep, breath-like explanatory sentence.
4. **Specific Micro-Stories (First-Person Observation):**
   - Use phrasing like *"In clinical observation..."*, *"When writing Stop Spiraling..."*, *"Watch what happens to your jaw right now as you read this."*
5. **Actionable 60-Second Interoceptive Reset:** Deliver genuine immediate relief in the text itself. Never hold back value; when readers experience physical relief from a free 60-second exercise, they immediately buy the book to get the other 15 protocols.

## PART 2: GOOGLE DISCOVER ALGORITHM & TECHNICAL RANKING RULES
Google Discover delivers millions of targeted mobile impressions without the user typing a single search query. Discover requires strict compliance with 5 core parameters:

### 1. High-Resolution Featured Image Requirements (Google Official)
- **Minimum Width:** Exactly `1200px` or wider.
- **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` widescreen (e.g., `1200 x 675 px` or `1600 x 900 px`).
- **HTML Meta Tag:**
  ```html
  <meta name="robots" content="index, follow, max-image-preview:large">
  ```
- **Image Style Rules for Discover Virality:**
  - ❌ **Forbidden:** Company logos, book cover flat graphics, sterile clinic stock photos (doctor with clipboard), generic text overlays.

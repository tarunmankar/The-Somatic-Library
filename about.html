About the Author

Practical Lived Experience & Body-First Protocols

For years, I lived trapped in my own nervous system — battling acute anxiety, sudden chest tightness, chronic overthinking, and the exhausting fog of mental burnout. Like many, I tried positive affirmations, mindfulness apps, and sheer willpower, but none of it silenced the bodily alarms. That frustration led me into a relentless deep dive into somatic psychology, polyvagal neuroscience, and interoception — discovering that you cannot simply think your way out of a survival reflex stored in the body.

I don't write from an academic pedestal or theoretical distance. Having spent years solving complex logical systems under high pressure, my approach was purely practical: understanding why the nervous system fires false alarms, and testing the exact, 60-second physiological resets that bring it back to safety. The Somatic Library was born directly from that lived experience — providing honest scripts and body-first tools so fellow overthinkers, high achievers, and sensitive bodies can finally feel grounded, calm, and sovereign in their own skin.
